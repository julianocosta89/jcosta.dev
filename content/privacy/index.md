This is a personal blog. It has no ads, sets no cookies of its own and only runs analytics if you agree.

## Analytics

If you agree, the site loads [Dash0 Website Monitoring](https://www.dash0.com/) so I can see
which pages people read, how fast they load and whether anything breaks. With your consent it collects:

- page views, the page URL and title, and the referring page
- page performance (load times, Core Web Vitals), JavaScript errors and repeated "rage" clicks
- your browser's user agent string and window size
- a recording of about 1 in 10 visits, showing scrolling and clicks, with every form input masked

To group events into a visit, the script stores a random session identifier in your browser's
local storage (`d0_session`) and session storage. Nothing is loaded and nothing is stored until
you click **Accept**. If you decline, the site works exactly the same.

Your choice is kept in your browser for 6 months, then you'll be asked again.
